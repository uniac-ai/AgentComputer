Released under npm's `next` tag: `npm install @agentorg/computer@next` for the program, `npm install @agentorg/computer-sdk@next` for the SDK alone. `latest` stays 0.0.19 for the program. The images are `ghcr.io/uniac-ai/agentcomputer/computer:0.0.41` and `ghcr.io/uniac-ai/agentcomputer/service:0.0.41`.

## What 0.0.41 holds, since 0.0.40: #126, #127, #128, #129

- **An agent's call is acting while it runs,** and 10 seconds after it ends, in `presence` and `GET /v1/acting`. A call that ran longer than 10 seconds, or a turn whose calls came more than 10 seconds apart, showed nobody acting while the agent was at work.
- **What is published carries the notices of what it takes in.**
  - Each bundled package's `NOTICE` goes with its licence.
  - The service image carries the Docker client's licence and notice.
  - Both images carry an SBOM, and their `NOTICE` says where the source of their Debian packages under the GPL or LGPL is.
  - The Mac app carries its own licence and the notices of its program's crates.
- **The computer image leaves out ffmpeg,** which is only for recording video, and nothing here records video.
